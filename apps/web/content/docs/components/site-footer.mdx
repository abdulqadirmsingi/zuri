Use `SiteFooter` at the bottom of every page. Link columns fit themselves to the space, down to two columns at 320px.

## Installation

<InstallTabs name="site-footer" />

## Usage

```tsx
import { SiteFooter } from "@/components/ui/site-footer";

<SiteFooter
  brand="Kilele"
  description="Practical courses taught by people who do the work."
  columns={[{ title: "Learn", links: [{ label: "Courses", href: "/courses" }] }]}
  legal="© 2026 Kilele"
/>
```

## Accessibility

- A `<footer>` (contentinfo landmark). Each column is a `<nav>` named by its heading, so screen reader users can jump between them.
- Column titles are `h2` headings. If that does not fit your page outline, wrap or restyle as needed.
- Links are at least 24px tall and show a visible focus ring.
- Link text is the muted colour, which meets AA contrast on the page background.

## Tokens used

`--border`, `--muted-foreground`, `--foreground`, `--ring`.

## Known limitations

- It does not include social icons, a newsletter form or a language picker. Pass those in `brand` or `legal`, or add your own row.
